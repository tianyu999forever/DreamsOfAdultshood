# Mission3 hotel room plaques

Improves the 301, 302 and 303 interior identity signs, their original entrance labels, and the Lounge sign in Assets/Scenes/Mission3.unity at runtime. Preserves room numbers and names, places them on two readable lines, faces the player camera, and adds an owned dark backing with distance-dependent size. Nearby duplicate 301/303 legacy labels are hidden; the separate 302 entrance plaque remains. No collider is added. Walls still occlude labels normally.

The scene file, shared font and teammate assets remain unchanged. New materials and scripts are all inside this folder. The earlier Tutorial Room typography change was reverted, retaining the user's welcome text edits.
